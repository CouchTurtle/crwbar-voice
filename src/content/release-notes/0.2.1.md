## Long recordings, and audio files that actually import

A bugfix release. Two kinds of recording could not be transcribed at all: long
ones, and several common file formats.

### Long recordings no longer crash the app

Transcribing a 19-minute recording made the app quit without warning. The
speech engine reserves memory in proportion to the length of one request, and at
that size the request was over 12 GB — the reservation failed and took the app
down with it. Recordings are now transcribed in pieces of about two minutes and
stitched back together, with each cut placed at the quietest moment nearby so it
lands between words rather than inside one.

This applies to microphone recordings as much as to imported files, so any
recording long enough to hit the old limit now finishes.

### Voice memos from an iPhone

Recorders such as iPhone Voice Memos store the file's index _after_ the audio.
Reading one requires jumping to the end of the file, which the decoder was not
set up to do — so those recordings were rejected as an unknown format even
though nothing was wrong with them.

### Apple Lossless

Voice memos recorded with the Lossless setting use ALAC, which was not among the
bundled decoders. They now import like any other file.

### Picking a file

_General_ has a **Choose audio file…** button, so importing no longer depends on
hitting the recording bar with a drag. Files can also be dropped onto the main
window.

### Diagnostics

Failed imports are written to the log as well as shown in the app. Previously
the message vanished with the notification, leaving nothing to look at
afterwards.

Supported imports: WAV, MP3, M4A (AAC and ALAC), FLAC, OGG/OGA and OPUS,
including WhatsApp voice notes.
