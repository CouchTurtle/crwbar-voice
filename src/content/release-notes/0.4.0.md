## History that keeps everything, and finds it again

### New

- **Every transcript is kept.** History no longer deletes old entries, only their
  audio. The newest 10 recordings keep theirs _(Advanced → History)_.
- **Search** across all transcripts. Matches are highlighted; word order and
  case don't matter, umlauts included.
- **`.caf` files** transcribe — AAC, Opus, Apple Lossless, PCM and IMA ADPCM
  all tested.
- **Keep recording after release** _(General)_ — up to 2 s longer, so the last
  word isn't cut off. Off by default.

### Improved

- The last moment of a recording is no longer dropped — most noticeable with
  Bluetooth microphones. Fix from upstream Handy.
- File transcriptions are labelled **Upload** in History.
- Entries without audio say so, and why.

### Good to know

- Entries the old limit already deleted can't be restored.
- If you kept the old default of 5, it moves to 10.
