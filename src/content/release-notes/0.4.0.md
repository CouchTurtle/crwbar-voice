## Every transcript kept, and found again

### History keeps everything you said

History used to delete whole entries — the text along with the recording —
beyond the newest five. Now every transcript is kept for good. Only audio is
limited: the newest 10 recordings keep theirs, which you can change under
_Advanced_. Older entries show a short note instead of a player, and starred
entries keep their audio as before.

Entries the old rule already deleted cannot be brought back; from this version
on, nothing more is lost.

### Search

History has a search box. Every word you type has to appear in the transcript,
in any order and regardless of case — umlauts included, so "änderung" finds
"Änderung".

### Microphone and Upload

Transcriptions from files are now labelled **Upload** in History.

### Core Audio Format files

`.caf` files transcribe, whatever codec they carry — AAC, Opus, Apple Lossless,
PCM or IMA ADPCM. Those the built-in decoder cannot read go through macOS's own
audio converter.

### The last word is no longer clipped

When a recording ended, audio that had already been captured was thrown away
in two places — a few milliseconds each, more with Bluetooth microphones. Both
are fixed.

A word can also be cut off because the shortcut was released while you were
still saying it. For that, _General_ now has **Keep recording after release**,
which records up to two seconds longer. It is off by default, and transcription
starts that much later.

Supported imports: WAV, MP3, M4A (AAC and ALAC), FLAC, OGG/OGA, OPUS and CAF,
including WhatsApp voice notes and Apple Voice Memos.
