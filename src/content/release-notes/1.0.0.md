## A new look, and a History that keeps everything

### New

- **A calmer design.** Settings sit in cards, and every option explains itself
  right under its name. The page title and the model in use stay at the top;
  shortcuts show as keys (⌥ Space).
- **Liquid Glass** behind the recording bar on macOS 26.
- **History keeps every transcript**, grouped by day, and searches all of them
  with the matches highlighted. The newest 10 recordings keep their audio
  _(Advanced → History)_.
- **Models show what each one is for** — _Dictation_ or _Files_ — and downloads
  show their progress in place.
- **`.caf` files** transcribe, AAC, Opus and Apple Lossless included.
- **Keep Recording After Release** _(General)_ — up to 2 s longer, so the last
  word isn't cut off. Off by default.

### Fixed

- The end of a recording is no longer dropped, most noticeable on Bluetooth.
- Esc works in other apps again after a short recording.
- With _Lower Audio While Recording_ on, a crash mid-recording no longer leaves
  your Mac quiet.
- With _AI_ off, nothing reaches an AI service — re-transcribing included.
- Transcripts are no longer written to the log file.

### Good to know

- _Re-transcribe_ and _Delete_ are in each History entry's **…** menu.
- Entries the old limit already deleted can't be restored. If you kept the old
  default of 5, it is now 10.
- After updating, allow crwbar voice under _Accessibility_ again.
